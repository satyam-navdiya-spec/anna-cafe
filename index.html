Our Story

Welcome to Anna Cafe

Anna Cafe was created with one simple idea — bring the authentic taste of South India to every table.

From crispy dosas and soft idlis to delicious vadas and aromatic filter coffee, our menu is inspired by traditional South Indian food.

We believe good food does not need to be complicated. It needs fresh ingredients, traditional recipes and the right amount of love.

  • Authentic South Indian flavours
  • Freshly prepared food
  • Quality ingredients
  • Warm and friendly atmosphere
